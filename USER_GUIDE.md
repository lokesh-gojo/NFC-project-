## 1. Quick Start for Business Owners

### Step 1: Account Registration & Onboarding
1. Visit `/auth/register` and enter your name, email, business name, and category.
2. Complete the 6-step Onboarding Wizard:
   - **Step 1:** Enter business title, tagline, currency, and short bio.
   - **Step 2:** Provide logo and cover image URLs.
   - **Step 3:** Enter phone, WhatsApp number, and address.
   - **Step 4:** Add your official Google Maps Review URL.
   - **Step 5:** Enter your guest Wi-Fi SSID and password.
   - **Step 6:** Launch your profile to generate your unique link: `nexora.in/b/your-slug`.

---

## 2. Managing Your Digital Menu (`/dashboard/menu`)

### Creating Categories
1. Click **Add Category**.
2. Give it a name (e.g. *Specialty Coffees*, *All-Day Brunch*, *Pastries*).
3. Reorder or delete categories as your seasonal menu evolves.

### Adding Products
1. Click **Add Menu Item**.
2. Fill in the product name, price, description, and high-resolution photo URL.
3. Toggle dietary tags: **Vegetarian**, **Popular / Chef's Pick**, or add allergen tags (e.g., *Dairy*, *Gluten*, *Nuts*).
4. Click **Save Product**.

### Live Stock Toggles
If an item sells out mid-service, click the **Available** pill on that product card. It will immediately switch to **Sold Out** on your customer menu without requiring a page refresh.

## 3. Printing QR Stands & Programming NFC Tags (`/dashboard/qr-nfc`)

### Generating Dynamic QR Table Tents
1. Navigate to **QR Codes & NFC Tag Engine**.
2. Click **Create Smart Link** and select **QR Code**.
3. Enter a label (e.g. *Table 1 Stand*, *Patio Booth 3*).
4. Select the preview on the right.
5. Click the **Print** icon to produce a print-ready table tent with your business name and auto-generated QR code.

### Programming Physical NFC Tags
1. Order standard NTAG213 / NTAG215 NFC stickers, discs, or acrylic stands.
2. In your Nexora dashboard, click **NFC Tap Tags** tab and click the **Copy** icon next to your tag's destination URL (e.g. `https://nexora.in/go/nfc-counter`).
3. Download the free **NFC Tools** app on your iPhone or Android device.
4. In NFC Tools:
   - Select **Write** → **Add a record** → **URL / URI**.
   - Paste the copied short link.
   - Tap **Write** and hold your phone against the NFC sticker.
5. Place the tag on your tables, counter, or mirrors. When customers tap their phone against it, your business profile will pop open on their screen!

## 4. Guest Wi-Fi Portal (`/dashboard/wifi`)
1. Enter your venue's Wi-Fi network name (SSID) and password.
2. Select encryption type (WPA/WPA2 is standard).
3. The dashboard creates a native Wi-Fi QR code. Customers simply point their native phone camera at the code to join your network in one tap.

---

## 5. Reviewing Customer Feedback (`/dashboard/feedback`)
1. Customers can rate their experience (1 to 5 stars) and submit private suggestions.
2. Review new incoming feedback in your dashboard feed.
3. Filter by rating or topic (Food Quality, Service, Cleanliness, Ambience).
4. Click **Export CSV Report** to download records for team meetings.

---

## 6. Staff & Team Roles (`/dashboard/team`)
Invite your managers and baristas:
- **Owner**: Full administrative access and billing.
- **Admin**: Can adjust menus, create QR/NFC links, and manage Wi-Fi.
- **Staff**: Can toggle item availability and view incoming orders.
